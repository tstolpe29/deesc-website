      What is available today?

      The brief builder and comparison canvas work publicly in your browser. The AI workspace is an owner-only private beta, not a public customer service or a product currently offered for sale. Customer onboarding and billing are not available.

      The AI workspace supports registered OpenAI, Claude, Ollama and OpenAI-compatible connections. API keys stay encrypted on the connected Windows PC. The PC must be awake and signed in. The private host stores briefs and replies; selecting Ollama does not make the hosted workspace local-only.

      Selected providers receive your brief and excerpts. Later rounds also receive other reviewers’ replies. API usage can cost money. Agents cannot execute code, access files or control your PC. Agreement is not independent verification.
